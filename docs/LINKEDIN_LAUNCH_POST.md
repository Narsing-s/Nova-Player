# Nova Player — LinkedIn launch post

## Ready-to-publish post

🎬 **Introducing Nova Player — an offline-first Android video player**

I’m building Nova Player to make watching videos stored on your device simple and comfortable, with a modern interface and practical playback controls.

✨ **What Nova Player focuses on**
- Offline playback of videos stored on your device
- Playback controls and gesture-based interaction
- Subtitle and audio-track options
- Playback speed and resume progress
- A dark, clean interface designed for everyday use

Nova Player is an independent project that I’m continuing to improve. I’d love to hear from Android users, movie lovers, and developers about what would make it more useful.
